Last Bastion · Player information

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026 · Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hearin's Brew ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in Last Bastion, our mobile game, and how third-party services may process information when their features are enabled.

Development status: Last Bastion is in development. Its current save system stores progress locally on the device. Google Play Games cloud saving and Google AdMob advertising are planned features and are not described here as currently active. We will update this policy before enabling new services that change how player data is handled.

1. Information the game uses

Last Bastion stores game progress and preferences on your device, such as Core Shards, unlocked upgrades, achievements or statistics where implemented, game settings, and timestamps used to calculate offline progress. This information is used to operate the game and restore your progress. Local game data is not automatically an online account.

Depending on the version and services enabled, the app or its service providers may also process technical information needed for app operation, purchases, crash diagnostics, sign-in, or advertisements. The exact data involved depends on the feature and provider.

2. Google Play purchases

Last Bastion is being prepared to offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. If purchases are enabled, Google processes payment information under Google's own policies. We do not need to receive your full payment-card number. The game and a future verification service may receive purchase tokens, product identifiers, transaction status, and related information needed to verify purchases, provide entitlements, prevent duplicate grants, and restore eligible purchases.

Purchases are not considered fulfilled until the applicable verification and entitlement systems are operational. The availability of products may vary by app version and region.

3. Google Play Games and cloud saves (planned)

We plan to offer optional Google Play Games sign-in and cloud saves. If introduced and you choose to sign in, Google may provide a player identifier and related profile information permitted by the service. Game progression and preferences may then be associated with that identifier so they can be synchronized across devices. Cloud synchronization will require additional integration and will not be active solely because the game is installed.

If cloud saving is introduced, we will explain how to manage conflicting saves and how to request deletion of cloud-associated game data.

4. Advertising (planned)

We plan to offer optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. If ads are enabled in a future version, Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers where available, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, and other information described in Google's advertising disclosures. Consent and privacy choices will be provided where required. We will update this policy and the game's disclosures before ads become active.

5. How information is used and shared

Information is used to run the game, maintain saved progress, provide optional features, process and verify purchases, prevent abuse, and support players. Where third-party features are enabled, necessary information may be processed by the relevant providers, including Google Play, Google Play Games, and Google AdMob. We do not claim that all of these services are active in the current version.

We do not sell player information. Third-party providers may process information according to their own policies and applicable privacy choices.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until it is cleared through available device or game controls, or the app's data is removed. Uninstalling the game may remove locally stored progress, depending on your device's backup behavior.

If online accounts or cloud saving are introduced, associated records may be retained as needed to operate those services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and prevent fraud. To ask about your information or request deletion of game-associated data we control, contact hearinsbrew@gmail.com. We may need enough information to locate and verify the relevant records. Google-managed account, purchase, and advertising data may also need to be managed through Google's own privacy controls.

7. Children and family privacy

Last Bastion is not currently presented as a service specifically directed to children. We do not intentionally request personal information from children through an in-game registration form. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the request. Any future advertising or account features will be configured according to the audience selected for the game and applicable platform requirements.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by our systems. However, no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not share passwords or payment-card information with us by email.

9. Third-party privacy information

Google services, when used, operate under their own terms and privacy practices. You can review Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Last Bastion develops or new services become available. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised update date. Material changes will be communicated where required.
